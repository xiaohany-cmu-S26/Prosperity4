SECTION 1. PRODUCT UNIVERSE

    VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT   underlying for the option chain (call it VFE below)

    VEV_5300              slightly OTM

    VEV_5500              OTM
    VEV_6000              floor-priced 0.50, no real liquidity for edge
    VEV_6500              floor-priced 0.50, no real liquidity for edge

Time-to-expiry (TTE) bookkeeping:
    Round 3 starts at TTE = 5 days.
    Within a round, timestamp goes 0 -> 999900 step 100.
    1,000,000 timestamps = 1 day.
    tte_days(day_idx, timestamp) = (5 - day_idx) - timestamp / 1_000_000
    Detect new sub-day by timestamp reset (current_ts < last_ts).

SECTION 3. VOLATILITY — NAIVE vs. NOISE-CORRECTED
================================================================================

Critical: the naive tick-level realized vol of VFE is misleading. Microstructure
noise (bid-ask bounce) inflates short-horizon variance.

Signature plot of VFE (sigma_per_day at different sampling strides):

  stride k     std per step      sigma per day
       1       0.000215          0.0216
       5       0.000416          0.0186
      10       0.000574          0.0182
      50       0.001280          0.0181
     100       0.001726          0.0173
     500       0.003390          0.0152
    1000       0.003066          0.0097
    5000       0.003125          0.0044

Reading: variance falls monotonically with sampling interval, classic Roll/
Hasbrouck noise signature. The lag-1 return autocorrelation of -0.16 confirms
bid-ask bounce.

The TRUE diffusion vol of the underlying is ~1.0% to 1.5% per day, NOT 2.16%.

The chain implies vol of 1.20% to 1.32% per day. Therefore the chain is
approximately FAIRLY priced in a vol sense — it is NOT a screaming long-vol
opportunity.

For a noise-corrected estimator, use two-scale realized variance (TSRV):

  rv_fast = variance of returns at stride 1 over window N
  rv_slow = variance of returns at stride k over window N
  Daily TSRV variance = (rv_slow * 10000/k) - (rv_fast * 10000) * (k/N)
  Daily TSRV vol      = sqrt(max(TSRV_variance, eps))

Recommended parameters: N=2000, k=100. EMA-smooth the result with alpha=0.10.

SECTION 6. STATISTICAL ARBITRAGE — VIABILITY AND TIERS
================================================================================

Stat arb is viable. Three flavors, in order of edge-to-risk:

--- TIER S: deep-ITM basis arbitrage ---
For VEV_4000 and VEV_4500:
  Theoretical:  C(K) = max(VFE - K, 0) since delta=1 and vega=0.
  Empirical basis deviation: up to ±7 ticks, std=0.83.

Trade: passive bids at (VFE - K) - 1, asks at (VFE - K) + 1, sized 20 each side.
Take when ask < (VFE-K) - 2 or bid > (VFE-K) + 2.
Risk: nearly riskless given fills. Reward: tick-by-tick, very high turnover
when active. Currently underexploited (only 4 fills each over 3 days in
backtest 447866 — should be hundreds).

--- TIER A: vol relative-value via smile dip ---
The 5300/5400/5500 butterfly:
  Fly value = C(5300) - 2*C(5400) + C(5500)
  Currently ~21-22 ticks vs flat-smile fair of ~16-17.
  Direction: the fly is rich because the body (5400) is too cheap.
  Trade: sell the fly = short 1x C(5300), long 2x C(5400), short 1x C(5500).
  Vega-neutral by construction; delta-neutralize by sizing.

Equivalent: buy K=5400 vol via take-side at the bid (consistent with the
100%-below-mid trade flow), short K=5300/5500 vol on the ask side.

--- TIER B: HYDROGEL_PACK passive MM ---
Independent stationary process. Modal inside spread is 16 ticks. If you sit
inside at FV ± 5 with a 1000-tick EMA fair value, you collect roughly 5-6
ticks per round-trip, with mean reversion converting positions to PnL on the
~500-1000 tick autocorrelation horizon.

--- TIER B: VFE microstructure MM ---
Tight 5-tick spread, deep book (~38 each side). Less edge per fill than
HYDROGEL_PACK but higher turnover. Critical: this product also serves as
the delta hedge for the option book. Quoting must reconcile MM PnL goals
with hedging mandates.

--- NOT VIABLE ---
VEV_6000/6500 lottery: at floor 0.50 with TTE=5d and sigma=1.3%/day, P(land
above strike) is well under 1%. EV essentially zero.
VEV_4500/5000/5100 making: dead books, expect <1 fill per day.

  Product               Inv     PnL      Notes
  HYDROGEL_PACK         +200   -6875     max long, biggest loss
  VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT   +147   -4849     long-into-falling-market
  VEV_4000                +3    +134
  VEV_4500                +3     +99
  VEV_5000                -1     +28
  VEV_5100              -216   -1122
  VEV_5200              -300    -704     max short
  VEV_5300              -300     +80     max short
  VEV_5400              -300     +96     max short  <- WRONG, should be long
  VEV_5500              -300    -145     max short
  VEV_6000                 0       0
  VEV_6500                 0       0

Aggregate option delta (using fair vols at S=5295, T=5d):
  +3*(1.0) + 3*(1.0) + (-1)*(0.98) + (-216)*(0.91) + (-300)*(0.74) +
  (-300)*(0.49) + (-300)*(0.24) + (-300)*(0.10)
  ≈ +3 + 3 - 1 - 196 - 222 - 147 - 72 - 30 = -662

Plus VFE position: -662 + 147 = -515 net VFE-equivalent delta.

The book is structurally un-hedgeable: VFE position limit is ±200 but the
options book carries -515 delta. Even if VFE were forced to +200, residual
delta is -315 with no instrument to hedge it.

Root causes:

(1) NO DELTA HEDGING. The Trader.run() loop processes options and underliers
    independently. Greeks are not computed. There is no mechanism to neutralize
    chain delta against the VFE book.

(2) HYDROGEL_PACK FV biased high. Configured anchor_fv=10000 with
    ema_anchor_weight=0.30, but empirical mean is 9990. FV is biased up by
    ~3 ticks. Combined with weak skew (kicks in only at |pos|>=100), quotes
    drift up while price falls -> get filled long -> can't sell out.

(3) Skew tiers far too gentle. (100, 160, 200) -> (0.0, 0.015, 0.03) factors.
    By the time skew engages at |pos|=100, half the position limit is already
    consumed and fills are still cheaper than current price. End position
    +200 confirms skew never bent quotes hard enough.

(4) Smile mispriced. Global OPTION_SIGMA = 0.20 ignores structural smile.
    K=5400 is 6-10 bps cheaper than chain average; backtest sold all three
    of K=5300/5400/5500 to limit indiscriminately. Should have been NET LONG
    K=5400, NET SHORT K=5300 and K=5500.

(5) Deep-ITM basis untraded. VEV_4000 and VEV_4500 had 4 fills each — the
    cleanest edge in the round was almost completely missed. Quoting must
    be much more aggressive on these strikes.

(6) Inactive strikes set to do_make=True. VEV_5000 had only 9 trades total
    in entire historical data; do_make=True consumes nothing but contributes
    nothing. Acceptable, but realize these will not build positions.


================================================================================
SECTION 8. VOLATILITY ENGINE DESIGN
================================================================================

Single most important component. Three vol estimates tracked simultaneously:

  sigma_K       per-strike implied vol from market mid (active strikes only)
  sigma_chain   vega-weighted consensus across active strikes (master anchor)
  sigma_RV      noise-corrected realized vol of underlying (regime indicator)

Class signature:

  class VolEngine:
      def update(self, vfe_mid, tte_days, option_mids, option_spreads):
          ...
      def fair_vol(self, K) -> float           # for pricing per-option BS
      def chain_iv(self) -> float              # current consensus
      def realized_vol(self) -> float          # noise-corrected daily
      def vol_premium(self) -> float           # chain - realized; signed
      def to_dict(self) / from_dict()          # JSON state persistence

Internal state (all persisted via traderData):

  iv_ema[K]        per-strike IV EMA, alpha=0.05
  chain_ema        chain consensus IV EMA, alpha=0.02 (steady state)
                                            alpha=0.20 during bootstrap
  smile_bias[K]    EMA of (iv_ema[K] - chain_ema), alpha=0.02
  mid_history      deque maxlen=2000 of recent VFE mids
  bootstrap_count  int; while < 500, use bootstrap alphas
  rv_ema           EMA of two-scale realized vol estimate, alpha=0.10

Per-tick update procedure:

  (a) Append vfe_mid to mid_history. If len < 200, return early with defaults
      chain_iv=0.0125, realized_vol=0.013.

  (b) For each K in {5000, 5100, 5200, 5300, 5400, 5500}:
        Solve sigma_K = implied_vol(option_mids[K], vfe_mid, K, tte_days)
        If sigma_K is None or |sigma_K - iv_ema[K]| > 3*recent_std: skip.
        Update iv_ema[K] = (1-0.05) * iv_ema[K] + 0.05 * sigma_K

  (c) Compute chain consensus weights:
        weight[K] = vega(vfe_mid, K, tte_days, iv_ema[K])
        Down-weight wide-spread strikes: weight[K] *= 5 / max(spread[K], 5)
        Exclude K where vega < 50 (TTE collapse on far OTM)
        chain_iv_raw = sum(weight[K] * iv_ema[K]) / sum(weight[K])
        Update chain_ema with appropriate alpha (bootstrap vs steady).

  (d) Update smile biases:
        smile_bias[K] = (1-0.02) * smile_bias[K] + 0.02 * (iv_ema[K] - chain_ema)

  (e) Two-scale realized variance:
        Let H = list(mid_history)
        rv_fast = variance of log returns at stride 1 over last min(2000, len(H))
        rv_slow = variance of log returns at stride 100 over same window
        N = effective window length
        var_daily = (rv_slow * (10000/100)) - (rv_fast * 10000) * (100/N)
        rv_raw = sqrt(max(var_daily, 1e-8))
        Update rv_ema = (1-0.10) * rv_ema + 0.10 * rv_raw

Initial values (use as cold-start priors):

  chain_ema:        0.01270  (chain mean from days 0-2)
  rv_ema:           0.01270  (start with chain implied)
  iv_ema[K]:        chain_ema + smile_bias_prior[K]
  smile_bias_prior:
      5000: -0.00007
      5100: -0.00020
      5200:  0.00000
      5300: +0.00020
      5400: -0.00060
      5500: +0.00060

Public method semantics:

  fair_vol(K)      = clamp(chain_ema + smile_bias[K], 0.005, 0.05)
  chain_iv()       = chain_ema
  realized_vol()   = rv_ema
  vol_premium()    = chain_ema - rv_ema   (positive = options rich vs realized)

Hyperparameter caveat: the alphas (0.05, 0.02, 0.10) are conservative defaults.
DO NOT tune them aggressively to historical data — they are the most overfittable
component. If tuning, use day 0+1 only, validate on day 2.


================================================================================
SECTION 9. DELTA HEDGING ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

The single most important fix from backtest 447866. Required logic:

(1) After all option orders are formed (but before VFE orders), compute:

      net_delta = position[VFE]
                + sum over K { position[VEV_K] * delta(VFE_mid, K, T, fair_vol_K) }
                + sum over option ORDERS pending this tick
                  { order.qty * delta(...) }

    Including pending orders is critical — without it the hedger fights itself.

(2) Hedge thresholds (apply in order):

      |net_delta| <= 10:   no hedge action.
      |net_delta| 10-30:   passive limit at 1 tick inside best on side that
                           reduces |net_delta|. Size = ceil(|net_delta|).
      |net_delta| 30-100:  cross the spread, take liquidity to bring |net_delta|
                           down toward 30. Size = |net_delta| - 30.
      |net_delta| > 100:   aggressive market take. Size = |net_delta| - 30.

(3) Delta budget — pre-trade option sizing:

    Before placing any option order, compute:
       expected_post_delta = current_net_delta + order.qty * delta(K)
    If |expected_post_delta| > 180, reduce order size or skip the order.
    The 180 leaves 20 ticks of headroom for VFE limit (200).

(4) HYDROGEL_PACK is independent. It is NOT in the delta computation. It has
    its own ±200 limit and is hedged only by its own mean reversion.

================================================================================
SECTION 11. VOL-PREMIUM POSITION TILT
================================================================================

After per-strike pricing and delta-budgeting, apply a chain-wide tilt based on
vol_premium = chain_iv - realized_vol:

  vp > +0.001:   options chain is RICH vs realized. Bias toward selling vol.
                 sell_cap_multiplier = 1.0 + min(vp/0.002, 1.0)   (up to 2x)
                 buy_cap_multiplier  = max(1.0 - vp/0.002, 0.5)

  vp < -0.001:   options chain is CHEAP vs realized. Bias toward buying vol.
                 buy_cap_multiplier  = 1.0 + min(-vp/0.002, 1.0)
                 sell_cap_multiplier = max(1.0 + vp/0.002, 0.5)

  |vp| <= 0.001: neutral. Both multipliers = 1.0.

These multipliers scale how much of the available position-limit budget gets
consumed in each direction per tick. They do NOT change pricing — pricing always
uses fair_vol(K). They change appetite, not fair value.

This serves as a regime-shift circuit breaker: if realized vol spikes, chain
becomes "cheap vs realized" relative to where it was, vp flips negative, the
bot stops loading short vol, and starts buying it. This is exactly the right
behavior in a vol regime change.

================================================================================
APPENDIX B. WHAT NOT TO DO
================================================================================

(1) Do NOT use a global hardcoded sigma. The previous OPTION_SIGMA = 0.20 is
    the root cause of the smile-misreading errors. All vol values flow from
    VolEngine.fair_vol(K).

(2) Do NOT trade 6000/6500. Floor-priced, no edge.

(3) Do NOT skip delta hedging when the options book is "small" — the threshold
    is not on number of positions but on net delta. A single -300 position on
    K=5200 alone exceeds the entire VFE hedge capacity.

(4) Do NOT anchor HYDROGEL_PACK FV at 10000. Empirical mean is 9990. Either
    use 9990 explicitly or use pure EMA without anchor.

(5) Do NOT make markets on dead books (4500/5000/5100) with size > 5. Quotes
    consume nothing visible to the matching engine but eat your position-limit
    headroom if they happen to fill.

(6) Do NOT include HYDROGEL_PACK in the delta calculation. It is uncorrelated
    with VFE (rho=0.006 between returns).

(7) Do NOT use scipy. Pure-Python brentq for IV solving (50 iterations on
    [1e-5, 5.0] is more than enough).

(8) Do NOT tune EMA alphas to backtest PnL. They are the single most
    overfit-prone component. The values above are conservative defaults and
    should be left alone unless there is principled reason to change.

(9) Do NOT remove existing FactorEngine or extreme_release / trend_abort
    scaffolding from 447866.py. Leave them in place (use_factors=False is
    fine), but do not let them fight the new delta hedger.

(10) Do NOT print to stdout in tight loops. The log line per 1000 timestamps
     is sufficient and deterministic.
